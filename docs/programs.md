# What 3D brings to your programs

**Programs you may already use, now with real depth.**
Each one shows its 3D on any of the registered outputs: a 3D TV or projector, an ordinary monitor with red-cyan glasses, and soon a VR headset or the phone you already own, in a cardboard viewer.
For most people, that phone will be their first 3D screen.

The words in quotes are each project's own description of itself; the line after it is what the edition adds.
**Coming** marks work that is done and measured but not yet in the edition's packages; **planned** marks work not built yet.

## Watch

**VLC**, *"the open-source multimedia player"*
3D films play in 3D, with the eyes in the right order even when the file stores the right eye first.

**Haruna**, *"Media player"*
The film plays in 3D while the controls, subtitles and playlist stay sharp and flat.

**mpv**, *"a free, open source, and cross-platform media player"*
It reads the 3D layout from the video itself, so a 3D file simply plays in 3D.

**Bino**, *"a video player with a focus on 3D and Virtual Reality"*
360° and 180° VR films on any of the registered outputs, flat or in 3D: on a screen, the mouse turns your view instead of your head.

**PlasmaTube**, *"Watch YouTube videos"*
3D videos from YouTube play in 3D, and 360° and 180° ones become a view you turn.

**Dragon Player**, *"Play video files and streams"*
Still simple: open a 3D file and it plays in 3D, with Swap Eyes for files that never say which eye is which.

**Plank Player**, Plasma Bigscreen's player for the couch and the remote control
3D films in 3D on the big screen, driven from the sofa.

**Gwenview**, *"Image Viewer"*
3D photos from 3D cameras and the 3D phones of 2011 open in 3D, as they were taken.

## Create

**Krita**, *"Digital Painting, Creative Freedom"*
Paint with real depth on a 3D canvas, and in 16 bits per colour instead of 8, so a soft gradient stays soft.

**Kdenlive**, *"Video editor"*
Edit a 3D video like any other: the monitors show it in 3D, and the finished file is still 3D.

**digiKam** and **Showfoto**, *"Photo Management Program"* and *"Image Editor"*
Browse, compare on the light table and edit 3D photographs, all in 3D.

**KolourPaint**, *"Paint Program"*
Touch up a 3D picture and save it still 3D.

**Spectacle**, *"Take screenshots and recordings"*
A screenshot of a 3D window keeps both eyes and is saved as a 3D photo.

**StorySplat**, in the browser, *"Turn your 3D scans into interactive tours"*
Tell stories inside 3D scans of real places, and show them in 3D with its 3D View.

## Learn, study and design

**Marble**, *"Virtual Globe"*
The Earth floats in front of the screen, with the stars far behind it.

**Kalzium**, *"Periodic Table of Elements"*
Molecules in real depth: see a molecule's shape instead of guessing it from shading.

**KAlgebra**, *"Graph Calculator"*
3D plots you can look into.

**Kubrick**, *"3-D Game based on Rubik's Cube"*
The cube, in 3D at last.

**GNU Octave**, *"Interactive programming environment for numerical computations"*
Any 3D figure in 3D, with two properties.

**Netgen**, the mesh generator
Its stereo option, which did nothing, now shows the mesh in 3D.

**Coming:** **OpenSCAD** (*"The Programmers Solid 3D CAD Modeller"*): see your part in 3D before you print it. **KiCad**: the circuit board in 3D before it is made. **F3D** (*"A fast and minimalist 3D viewer"*) and **GRASS GIS**: models and terrain in 3D.

## Play

**Half-Life 2** and **DOOM (2016)**
Both in 3D through their VR modes, DOOM even in an ordinary window on the desktop.

**Coming:** **KDE Game Controller**, every button, axis and hat of a flight stick, a wheel or pedals shown as the hardware really has them.
**Planned:** **MAME** and the emulators of the 3D consoles.

## The desktop itself

**KWin**, the window manager
A 3D window sits beside flat ones; each screen shows its own kind of 3D, two different 3D TVs at once included.

**KScreen**, the display settings
Your TV's 3D modes appear with its other modes, with a suggestion for games and one for films.

**KInfoCenter**, *"Learn about the system's hardware and status"*
See which 3D modes and colour depths your screen declares.
