# Merged outside Sparky Stereo

**For maintainers and distributions:** fixes and collaborative contributions to other projects, merged or in review, with links and dates.

**Sparky Stereo is also a campaign.**
Where a program breaks stereo or deep colour on the way to the screen, the fix goes to that program's own project, in its own style.
Each merge below was reviewed and accepted by that project's maintainers.
Each one shows the next project the same problem already fixed elsewhere, and Sparky Stereo is the desktop where all of them meet.

## Registered identifiers

**IANA registration, 20 October 2026:** [Matroska Track Plane Types](https://www.iana.org/assignments/matroska#matroska-track-plane-types) now assigns **3, 4 and 5** to the depth maps of the left-eye, right-eye and background planes, respectively, with Daniel Ramos as change controller and the [stereo-layer v1.9.0 specification](https://github.com/Sparky-OS/stereo-layer/blob/v1.9.0/docs/depth-marks.md) as reference.
This gives separate picture and depth tracks shared identifiers through `TrackCombinePlanes`, so readers can preserve their relationship before rendering stereo views.
**IANA registration, 26 October 2026:** [Matroska Stereo Modes](https://www.iana.org/assignments/matroska#matroska-stereo-modes) now assigns **15 through 20** to packed picture-and-depth layouts, following designated-expert review under the Specification Required procedure.
The six values cover picture and depth side by side or top and bottom in either order, four rows with graphics and graphics depth, and four quarters with background and background depth.
Daniel Ramos is the change controller, and the registry cites the same immutable stereo-layer v1.9.0 specification.
Assignment identifies the layouts; support in a muxer, decoder or player must be checked separately.

This registry milestone is separate from the software merges below.

- **x265, field-coding follow-up** ([#999](https://github.com/Multicorewareinc/x265/pull/999), merged by kirithika7 on 21 October 2026): Akilan Sivakumar removed the unnecessary `--b-adapt 0` restriction for `--field` and added regressions and input-layout documentation. Daniel reported the restriction during #986 review; our [48 combined encodes and 24 byte-identical field-input comparisons](https://github.com/Multicorewareinc/x265/pull/999#issuecomment-6278374499) supplied supporting validation. This is a collaborative report/testing contribution.
- **x265, frame packing** ([#986](https://github.com/Multicorewareinc/x265/pull/986), merged by kirithika7 on 22 October 2026 as [5143a2a](https://github.com/Multicorewareinc/x265/commit/5143a2a00ad0cc600831654a248b96ec7307eb4a), approved by Akilan Sivakumar, with a review by Vu Nguyen (vunguyen1989)): the `--frame-packing` option writes the H.265 frame packing arrangement SEI (ITU-T H.265, D.2.16), so an HEVC encode of side by side, top and bottom or frame sequential 3D video declares itself, as x264 already does.
- **PhotoCraft** ([#7815](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/pull/7815), merged by aiongg on 22 October 2026 as [8c92678](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/commit/8c92678a309404b141dce6ec52233cf2008151f4)): the two values of the PNG `sTER` chunk are named for what they mean, cross-fuse (right view first) and diverging-fuse (left view first), as [PNG Extensions 1.4.0, §3.6](https://www.libpng.org/pub/png/spec/register/pngext-1.4.0-pdg.html#C.sTER) defines them, so a caller can no longer build the eyes in the wrong order; the stored bytes do not change. It follows PhotoCraft's own fix from our report [#6063](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/issues/6063), [#6285](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/pull/6285) by mroxso, merged by Brandon Thomas (echelon) on 19 October 2026: a stereo PNG keeps its `sTER` chunk on save.
- **FFmpeg, VVC decoder** ([#25380](https://code.ffmpeg.org/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/pulls/25380), merged by Frank Plowman (frankplow) on 31 October 2026 as [502046ab](https://code.ffmpeg.org/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/502046abcfcbd4148fd60b442cc96a1bcad62fae), closing our report [#25379](https://code.ffmpeg.org/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/issues/25379)): when exporting a finished picture fails for lack of memory, the decoder no longer loses the picture it is decoding; the error is reported with the picture whose export failed. Our first change in FFmpeg.
