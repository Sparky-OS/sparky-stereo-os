# What people said

Sparky Stereo OS is checked by people who know stereo, in public, and this page keeps what they said.
Public words are quoted with the person's name and a link to where they said them.

## In public

> "I'm impressed by your presentation, Daniel."

Song Xu, co-chair of the W3C Media & Entertainment Interest Group, after the talk of 13 October 2026 ([minutes](https://www.w3.org/2026/10/13-me-minutes.html)).

> "Separate depth tracks with registered identifiers, and side-by-side HEVC that declares itself, are exactly what a weaver needs to stop guessing."

David Fattal, DisplayXR, on the Matroska depth-plane assignment and the x265 frame packing SEI ([DisplayXR #1928](https://github.com/DisplayXR/displayxr-runtime/issues/1928#issuecomment-6358015605)).

> "This is a thorough first drop. Zero off-table frames across 45 drags, and checking paint against weave origin entry by entry, is exactly the evidence we wanted."

David Fattal, DisplayXR, on our KWin window-geometry publisher ([DisplayXR #1928](https://github.com/DisplayXR/displayxr-runtime/issues/1928)).

> "STEREO3D.md is a clear read, and you've found the right seam."

David Fattal, on the KWin design ([DisplayXR discussion #1903](https://github.com/DisplayXR/displayxr-runtime/discussions/1903#discussioncomment-18858193)).

> "I'm excited to get to be a part of this. I love the vision!"

Jeromy Streets, author of KDE's video wallpaper plugin, which he moved into our organisation ([#78](https://github.com/Sparky-OS/sparky-stereo-os/issues/78)).

> "That's such a lovely story about the original team giving you access to the All Outputs license back in the day, and here you are contributing back to the open source code today!"

effcol, maintainer of wiz3D, the open continuation of iZ3D's stereo driver ([wiz3D #32](https://github.com/effcol/wiz3D/issues/32)).

> "It's really quite remarkable how much physical pop/push you appear to get with only a few pixels."

skyfishgoo, on the KDE Discuss thread about real depth on the desktop ([KDE Discuss](https://discuss.kde.org/t/real-depth-for-oceans-elevation-a-design-question-for-union/50744)).
