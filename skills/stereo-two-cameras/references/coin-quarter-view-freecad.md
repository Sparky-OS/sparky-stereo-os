# A Coin3D view in a Qt graphics view, fitted to the person's limits (FreeCAD)

**For** anyone giving a Coin3D (Open Inventor) program's 3D view a stereo pair on the edition when the view is Quarter's `QuarterWidget`, a `QGraphicsView` whose viewport is a `QOpenGLWidget`: which case it is, why upstream's quad-buffer entry did nothing, the route through the viewport's two buffers, Coin's own stereo camera and where it fails, a shear for orthographic eyes, the fit, picking, the overlays drawn over the scene, the options and what is told, the proofs and the traps.
Learned on FreeCAD 1.1.3 (Debian 1.1.3+dfsg-3, the edition's `1.1.3+dfsg-3+stereo3d1+lc1` and `+stereo3d2+lc1`, queue row 50 step 5, November 2026), on Coin 4.0.7, Stereo KWin 6.7.4 +stereo3d64, Mesa 26.1.6 +stereo3d29, qt6-base 6.11.2 +stereo3d23 and +stereo3d24, sparky-stereo-graphics 0.2.18 and libstereo-declare 1.12.2.

## Which case it is

Grep upstream's own tree, never our patch.
FreeCAD 1.1.3 has a View, Stereo menu (red/cyan, quad buffer, interleaved rows and columns, off) that sets Quarter's `SoRenderManager` stereo mode; upstream removed it on 2026-06-09 (pull request 30634, "Remove stereo views": the views did not work and nobody fixed them; issues 21496 and 27816), keeping Quarter's code.
Its quad-buffer entry draws nothing on the edition, and on any Qt 6 desktop: the viewport is a `QOpenGLWidget`, which renders into framebuffer objects, never into `GL_BACK_LEFT` and `GL_BACK_RIGHT` (Mesa logs `GL_INVALID_OPERATION` for the draw buffer), and its format never asks for stereo buffers.
So it is a scene where we add the cameras: fitted to the person's limits, no separation setting; FreeCAD's own "eye to eye distance" preference is not offered.

## The route

1. **The viewport's own format.** Quarter's `CustomGLWidget` takes the format the viewer is made with: set `setStereo(true)` there when Enable 3D is on, nowhere else, so with Enable 3D off the view is Debian's to the pixel. The edition's Qt shows a stereo `QOpenGLWidget` as a native window of its own (a subsurface on Wayland, a child window on xcb) and Mesa declares it; the rest of the window stays one picture.
2. **The paint event, not `paintGL()`.** A `QOpenGLWidget` used as a `QGraphicsView` viewport never gets its own paint event: `QAbstractScrollArea` routes it to the view, and Quarter draws in `QuarterWidget::paintEvent()` after `makeCurrent()`, then lets `QGraphicsView` paint with a `QPainter` over it. So Qt's stereo machinery (two `paintGL()` calls) never runs. Draw the frame twice there: `QOpenGLWidget::makeCurrent(QOpenGLWidget::LeftBuffer)`, the eye, the scene, the graphics view's painting; then the same with `RightBuffer`; then `makeCurrent(LeftBuffer)` again. `makeCurrent(TargetBuffer)` (Qt 6.5) sets the widget's current target buffer, and a `QPainter` on the viewport binds that buffer's framebuffer (`QOpenGLWidgetPaintDevice::ensureActiveTarget`), so the 2D painting follows the eye.
3. **The eyes.** Perspective: Coin's own stereo camera, `SoCamera::setStereoMode(LEFT_VIEW or RIGHT_VIEW)`, `setStereoAdjustment(e)`, `setBalanceAdjustment(C / focalDistance)`: in `SoCamera::GLRender()` the eye moves `e/2` along the camera's right vector and the frustum is skewed by `e/2 · near / C`, two parallel cameras meeting at the distance `C`. Orthographic: not Coin's (see the traps); a callback node right after the camera in the render manager's scene sets the eye's view volume, the camera's own moved by the inverse world shear `q - s ((q - P)·d - C) r` (`P` the camera, `d` its direction, `r` its right vector, `s = ±t/2`), and from it the cull, view volume, projection and viewing elements, as `SoCamera::GLRender()` does. `SbViewVolume::getMatrices()` inverts the volume's own frame, so the oblique volume gives exactly `x' = x + s (D - C)` on the screen and the same height; labels and annotations, which read the view volume element, follow their points.
4. **The limits.** libstereo-declare's buffer call in the viewport's buffer pixels: Wayland `stereo_depth_wayland_get_buffer_limits()` for the top-level window's `wl_surface` (`QPlatformNativeInterface::nativeResourceForWindow("surface", window()->windowHandle())`), X11 `stereo_depth_x11_get_buffer_limits()` on the viewport's own window (`winId()` of the stereo `QOpenGLWidget`, native on the edition's Qt), read four times a second; a change redraws the view. The library's header (Xlib's `Status`, `Bool`, `None`) is kept in a translation unit of its own. Qt's private native interface binds the package to its Qt: `libfreecad-python3` depends on `qt6-base-private-abi (= Qt's upstream version)`.
5. **The fit, by pixels.** Before the left eye the scene is drawn from the camera between the eyes into a single-sampled framebuffer of its own (the viewport may be multisampled, and its depth cannot be read then), and its depth is read twice: after the scene, and after FreeCAD's delayed annotations, which it draws over a cleared depth (`So3DAnnotation`). The same callback node records that pass's projection. With `A = P[2][2]`, `B = P[3][2]` (Coin's matrices are laid out as OpenGL's), a window depth `d` is the distance `B / ((2d - 1) + A)` in perspective and `(B - (2d - 1)) / A` in orthographic; `k` is half the buffer's width times `P[0][0]`. The separation `t` is the smallest of `popped / (k (C/Dnear - 1))` and `sunk / (k (1 - C/Dfar))` (orthographic: `popped / (k (C - Dnear))`, `sunk / (k (Dfar - C))`), `C` the camera's focal distance (Coin takes it at least at the near plane); with nothing off the focal plane, perspective puts infinity at the sunk limit and orthographic shows no depth.
5b. **A translucent pixel at its weighted mean (`1.1.3+dfsg-3+stereo3d2`).** Coin draws transparent shapes after the opaque ones with depth writes off (`SoGLRenderAction`, "disable writing into the z-buffer when rendering transparent objects", for FreeCAD's `SORTED_OBJECT_SORTED_TRIANGLE_BLEND`), so a depth read never sees them. The rule counts a translucent pixel at the contribution-weighted mean distance of what it shows. As KiCad's patch 0006 does it with the fog over the distance, but without touching a material: the measuring pass is drawn twice into a framebuffer with float colour (`GL_RGBA32F`, its format read back), with linear fog from the camera (start 0, end beyond the far plane), toward white in the first pass and toward black in the second, and the background cleared to the screen's distance over the fog's end in the first and to 0 in the second (in the eye node's callback, once per pass). The fog is the viewer's own `SoEnvironment` node's (`View3DInventorViewer::getEnvironment()`, `fogType = HAZE`, `fogColor`, `fogVisibility` = the fog's end), its fields changed with notification off and given back after the passes: Coin sets GL's fog from that node, which is traversed after the eye node, so a fog set with GL alone is switched off again (a trap below). Every fragment's own colour is the same in both passes, so the difference of the two red channels is, at every pixel, the fog's share blended exactly as the layers are: `sum(w_i d_i) / end`. A pixel whose difference is 0 where the depth shows a surface was drawn without the fog (a shader of its own) and counts at its depth; a pixel showing the background alone is not counted; an annotation drawn over a cleared depth counts where it hides the scene. Without a float framebuffer the pass is drawn once with `setDelayedObjDepthWrite(TRUE)`: transparent shapes count at their own distance, which keeps every pixel inside with less depth. The per-pixel rule is `stereoFitDistances()` (`src/Gui/View3DStereoFit`), unit-tested in `Gui_tests_run`. What a FreeCAD shape shows: a `Part` box draws its back face behind its front face, so a pixel of a box at transparency `T` is two layers, `a·d_front + (1-a)·a·d_back + (1-a)²·d_behind` with `a = 1 - T/100`; and a shape's Transparency is its faces' only: in the default "Flat Lines" mode its edges and vertices stay opaque, so its outline counts at its own distance and the shape keeps the depth it needs as an opaque one would ("Shaded" draws the faces alone).
5c. **The Framebuffer render type.** `View3DInventorViewer::setRenderType(Framebuffer)` draws the scene once into a framebuffer object and shows that picture on every redraw; nothing in FreeCAD 1.1.3 selects it but that call (`MouseSelection` chooses Image and Native, no preference or Python call reaches it). In stereo each eye's picture is drawn with its own camera after the fit (measured as a Native frame is) and kept in a framebuffer of its own; `renderFramebuffer()` shows the texture of the buffer Quarter made current (`currentTargetBuffer()`). The view's context is made current before the eyes are drawn: `setRenderType()` may be called outside a paint, and the measuring pass reads its depth in that context.
6. **Picking from the camera between the eyes.** The eyes exist only inside the paint event: Coin's pick actions use the camera's view volume, never `GLRender()`, and the camera's stereo mode is set back to mono after the right eye, so a click, the preselection highlight and the navigation all answer for the camera between the eyes, which is where the person fuses what is under the pointer.
7. **The overlays.** Drawn in window coordinates, once per eye at the same place, so at the screen's depth: the navigation cube (`NaviCube::drawNaviCube`), the corner coordinate system (`drawAxisCross`, its own viewport and frustum), the background gradient, the foreground root (its own orthographic camera), the rubber band and polygon of a selection (`GLGraphicsItem::paintGL`), the frame-rate label. In the scene, so at their own depth in each eye: the selection and preselection highlights, the axis cross at the origin (`Std_AxisCross`), measurement and dimension annotations.
8. **Options.** View, Stereo: "Enable 3D", the quad-buffer entry made to work (`Std_ViewIvStereoQuadBuff`, a check box, the preference `User parameter:BaseApp/Preferences/View/Enable3D`, on by default; `View3DSettings` applies a change to every open view by giving it a new viewport with the other format), and "3D Display Settings..." (`systemsettings kcm_kscreen`, else `kcmshell6 kcm_kscreen`, else told). The red/cyan and interleaved entries are not offered; Python's `setStereoType` keeps `Mono` and `QuadBuffer` for one view and refuses the others with a sentence; `listStereoTypes()` lists the two.
9. **Told once, in the report view** (`Base::Console().warning`), in the person's language through FreeCAD's own `.ts` catalogues: the limits not offered, not delivered after two seconds, not readable; the depth not readable; a view that got no stereo buffers on a desktop that offers them. A desktop without stereo buffers gets one picture and nothing said, unless the person turned Enable 3D on there.
10. **Trace for the proof:** `FREECAD_STEREO3D_TRACE=1` prints each frame's fit (limits, `k`, `C`, the nearest and farthest distances, the separation, the predicted disparities) and every sentence told, on standard error.

## The closed-form scene

Three `Part::Box` objects and a camera set from Python (`view.setCamera()` with an Inventor camera: the front view, `position 0 -200 0`, `orientation 1 0 0 1.5707964`, `focalDistance 200`, a perspective `heightAngle 0.39269908` or an orthographic `height 100`): a red box whose near face is at y = -30.5 (distance 169.5), a blue one at y = +19.5 (219.5) and a grey plate at y = +39 (239), the same distances as OpenSCAD's scene, so the numbers compare.
A plain grey background, and no navigation cube and corner coordinate system in these rows: their red, green and blue marks, the same in both eyes, would join the boxes' colour centroids.
The popped limit decides in every case (`popped / (C/169.5 - 1)` against `sunk / (1 - C/239)`), so the predictions do not depend on the view's size: perspective -4.000, +1.975, +3.627 pixels at 10 sunk and 4 popped, -8.000, +3.950, +7.255 at 20 and 8; orthographic -4.000, +2.557, +5.115 and -8.000, +5.115, +10.230.

## Traps

- **Quad buffer on a `QOpenGLWidget` draws nothing.** **Symptom:** View, Stereo, Quad Buffer changes nothing (queue row 724). **Cause:** a `QOpenGLWidget` draws into framebuffer objects, so Coin's `glDrawBuffer(GL_BACK_LEFT)` has no target, and the viewport's format never asked for stereo buffers. **Fix:** the route above. **How found:** FreeCAD's `CustomGLWidget` sets its format without `StereoBuffers`; Qt's documentation of stereo `QOpenGLWidget` (two `paintGL()` calls, `currentTargetBuffer()`).
- **A graphics view's viewport never gets `paintGL()`.** **Symptom:** a stereo format alone gives a declared window whose right eye is never drawn. **Cause:** `QAbstractScrollArea` takes the viewport's paint event (`viewportEvent()`), so `QOpenGLWidgetPrivate::render()`, which calls `paintGL()` once per buffer, never runs; Qt's own comment in `QOpenGLWidgetPaintDevice::ensureActiveTarget()` names this "used as a viewport" case. **Fix:** draw both buffers in the view's paint event with `makeCurrent(TargetBuffer)`, which sets the widget's current buffer, so a `QPainter` on the viewport follows it. **How found:** Qt 6.11's `qopenglwidget.cpp`, read in the published qt6-base source.
- **Coin's stereo camera bends an orthographic view.** **Symptom:** upstream's red/cyan view distorted orthographic views and "collapsed into a line" when zoomed out (issues 21496, 27553). **Cause:** `SoCamera::GLRender()` builds every stereo eye with `SbViewVolume::frustum()`, a perspective frustum, also for an orthographic camera. **Fix:** Coin's stereo for perspective only; orthographic eyes from the camera's own volume moved by the inverse world shear. **How found:** Coin 4.0.7's `src/nodes/SoCamera.cpp`; a small program against libCoin then checked that the moved volume gives `x + s (D - C)` within 1.2e-6 of device coordinates, the same height, and the same answer from `getMatrices()` and `projectToScreen()`.
- **A split view's panes stayed one picture.** **Symptom:** `Gui.createViewer(2)` given the document's scene graph showed the boxes in each pane with equal eyes; the trace said `fit limits=0 separation=0` and, before the scene was set, `pixels=0`. **Cause:** `setSceneGraph()` makes a new superscene in the render manager, and the eye node had been put into the old one, so the measuring pass recorded no projection. **Fix:** put the node after the camera of the current superscene at every frame (moved when it is elsewhere). **How found:** a pixel count and the bound framebuffer added to the trace for one run.
- **A mouse selection froze both eyes on the left picture.** **Symptom:** during a box selection (Shift+B, then a drag) the two eyes were equal over the whole view. **Cause:** FreeCAD's `MouseSelection` sets `View3DInventorViewer::Image`, which reads the bound framebuffer once (`grabFramebuffer()`, the left buffer) and draws that picture every frame. **Fix:** in stereo each eye's buffer is copied (a blit into a single-sampled framebuffer, so a multisampled viewport is read too) and each eye pass draws its own. **How found:** the band capture's 0 differing pixels in the 3D area, then `setRenderType()` in the viewer.
- **Turning Enable 3D off and on told "this view got no stereo buffers".** **Cause:** `setViewport()` can paint the new viewport at once, before the view's request was updated, so the check saw a stereo request on a viewport made without stereo. **Fix:** record the request, then replace the viewport. **How found:** the sentence in the menu and Python rows' traces right after the switch.
- **Debian's FreeCAD files with CRLF lines.** **Symptom:** a patch of a few hundred lines became 16,000. **Cause:** `CommandView.cpp`, `View3DPy.cpp`, `View3DInventor.cpp`, `View3DInventorViewer.h` and `Workbench.cpp` end their lines with CRLF; a script's `read_text()`/`write_text()` turned two of them into LF. **Fix:** edit with an editor that keeps the line ends, or write the bytes back with CRLF; check `grep -c $'\r$'` against `wc -l` for every changed file before making the patch.
- **A stereo viewport under alien ancestors reads a freed texture list when it moves.** **Symptom:** memcheck reports reads of a freed `QPlatformTextureList` (`count`, `flags`, `clipRect`, `geometry`, `texture`) inside `QPlatformBackingStore::rhiFlush()` whenever FreeCAD's layout changes (a dock, a second view, the close); none with Enable 3D off or on Debian's build. **Cause:** the edition's Qt patch that gives a stereo `QOpenGLWidget` its own window moves that window inside `QWidgetRepaintManager::flush()`; on Wayland the move delivers a synchronous expose, the nested `paintAndFlush()` rebuilds the top level's texture lists, and the outer flush reads the list it was given. **Fix:** in qt6-base +stereo3d24 (queue row 863), not in FreeCAD: the window is placed when an ancestor moves, with `QWindowContainer`'s technique, and upstream's d6d2a75cdbfe (a subsurface move asks for the parent's next frame) is carried; a move queued until the flush returned was measured too and left windows behind under load. FreeCAD's memcheck row on that Qt: see [the stereo QOpenGLWidget page](../../native-linux/references/qt-stereo-opengl-widget.md), which has the cause, the measurements and the small test that reaches the same read without FreeCAD. **How found:** memcheck of the stress row against the same run on Debian's build and with Enable 3D off, then the free's stack, then the patch's hunk in `qwidgetrepaintmanager.cpp`.
- **A transparent shape nearest the camera was not measured.** **Symptom:** the fit was the same with and without a translucent box in front. **Cause:** Coin's sorted transparent paths are drawn with depth writes off, so the depth read sees what is behind. **Fix:** the two fogged passes above. **How found:** `SoGLRenderAction.cpp` (`transpobjdepthwrite`, false by default) read in Coin 4.0.7's source.
- **Blackening the materials would make the shapes opaque.** **Symptom (avoided):** an overriding `SoMaterial` with black colours, to leave only the fog's colour, also overrides every shape's transparency. **Cause:** Coin, like SGI's Inventor, gives diffuse colour and transparency one override bit (`COIN_SEPARATE_DIFFUSE_TRANSPARENCY_OVERRIDE` separates them for the whole process). **Fix:** two passes fogged toward white and black: the shapes' own colours cancel in the difference. **How found:** `SoMaterial.cpp`'s comment on the override flags.
- **Fog takes the distance without its sign.** **Symptom:** in an orthographic view whose volume reaches behind the camera's plane, points behind it fold onto points in front. **Cause:** the fixed-function fog coordinate is `|z|` in eye space. **Fix:** move the view along its axis for the fog in the measuring pass (the viewing matrix by `-offset`, the projection by `+offset`, so the picture does not change) and take the offset off the mean. **How found:** KiCad's page in this folder (`wx-gl-canvas-and-grass-view.md`, 4b).
- **The fog set with GL was switched off.** **Symptom:** the trace said `unfogged=` every pixel (175,616 of 175,616), `translucent=0`, and a 50 % box in front of everything took no less depth than an opaque one. **Cause:** FreeCAD's scene root holds its own `SoEnvironment` node (fog none); Coin's `SoGLEnvironmentElement::updategl()` runs when that node is traversed, after the eye node's callback, and calls `glDisable(GL_FOG)` for fog none. **Fix:** set the fog on that node for the measuring passes (`HAZE`, linear from 0 to `fogVisibility`) and give its fields back after them, with notification off. **How found:** the trace's `unfogged` count on the first live run, then `SoGLEnvironmentElement.cpp` in Coin 4.0.7's source and `View3DInventorViewer::init()` (the environment is the scene root's second child).
- **A transparent box keeps its outline opaque.** **Symptom:** a box at 90 % transparency over the plate took the whole popped limit, on the old build and the new. **Cause:** FreeCAD's Transparency is the faces'; in "Flat Lines" the edges are opaque lines at the box's distance, and an opaque pixel counts at its own depth: that is the rule working. **Fix in the rig:** the weighting rows hide the box's edges (`DisplayMode = "Shaded"`), and one row keeps them and expects the opaque box's numbers. **How found:** looking at the capture: a faint box with a black outline.
- **A box shows two layers, not one.** **Symptom:** a 50 % box over the background measured 177.09 to 177.29, where a single layer gives 184.75. **Cause:** the box's back face (170.5) is drawn behind its front face (169.5): 0.5·169.5 + 0.25·170.5 + 0.25·200 = 177.375. **Fix:** predict with both faces. **How found:** the fog's own numbers in the trace, then the arithmetic. So a 50 % box over the plate is red on the build that leaves it out (its pixels at 187.125, -4.216 px with the plate's fit), and over the background -7.817 px; 90 % stays inside on both builds.
- **An edge seen end on has depth past its own end.** **Symptom:** the trace's nearest was 168.43 where the nearest face is at 169.5, on both builds, in one layout of the window (1654 x 843) and not in another (1534 x 843 gives 169.5; 1648 x 850 gave 169.493). **Cause:** the box's edge from its front corner (169.5) to its back corner (170.5) projects to under a pixel; its line fragments carry depth (and fog) extrapolated along that steep edge: 8 pixels at line width 2, 4 at width 1. The fit counts them, so the view gets about 3 % less depth than the closed form; no pixel passes a limit. **Fix:** none in this round (the separation errs on the safe side); the closed-form trace verdict takes the nearest at the face or within the box's 1-unit depth extent and predicts the farthest from that nearest. **How found:** a development trace of the nearest pixel's place (the box's corner in the capture, on the black outline), its fog distance agreeing with its depth, and the line width halving the count.
- **The Framebuffer render type, fitted outside a paint, read nothing.** **Symptom:** a pair at half its depth (red -1.998, blue +0.996); the trace `pixels=0` and the separation that puts infinity at the sunk limit. **Cause:** `setRenderType()` was called from Python, outside a paint, so the view's context was not current when the first eye's fit drew its measuring pass. **Fix:** make the viewport's context current before the eyes are captured. **How found:** the trace's pixel count in that row.
- **The Graphics page brings other fonts, and the layout moves.** sparky-stereo-graphics 0.2.18 depends on Plasma's workspace, which brings Noto and Hack fonts and Breeze's icons into a rig made from Debian's FreeCAD image: FreeCAD's 3D view becomes 1654 x 843 instead of 1648 x 850 (no library of FreeCAD's process changes). Predictions that do not depend on the view's size stay; anything sub-pixel (the trap above) moves.
- **The mean distance is not the mean disparity.** In perspective a disparity is linear in `1/d`, so the disparity of the mean distance (what the fog gives, as KiCad's patch) is not the weighted mean of the layers' disparities: -4.000 against -4.178 px for the 50 % box over the background (front -5.643, back -5.426, background 0). Reported; the rule is applied as KiCad's patch applies it.
- **The Framebuffer render type has no switch to test it with.** **Fix in the rig:** the view's C++ object from PySide (`shiboken6.getCppPointer(view.graphicsView())`, the viewer's first base) and the exported `Gui::View3DInventorViewer::setRenderType(RenderType)` through `ctypes` from `libFreeCADGui.so` (its path from `/proc/self/maps`).
- **The rig's own traps.** FreeCAD is started as `/usr/bin/freecad`, so its process name is `freecad`. Qt's xcb plugin dies (`QInputDevice::seatName()` from `QXcbConnection::populateTouchDevices()`) on the XInput2 event that follows KWin's fake-input pointer, Debian's FreeCAD as ours: on the xcb route move no pointer before the captures, and take the 3D area from the window tree (the viewport's own child window, of class `freecad`). Ctrl+Q does nothing while the navigation holds the mouse buttons (Debian's too): close the window through KWin during the drag, after clearing the GUI document's own modified flag (`Gui.ActiveDocument.Modified = False` after `saveAs()`), or a save dialog stops the close. FreeCAD draws without antialiasing, so each eye's edges move by whole pixels (orthographic +2.557 measures +2.0, +5.115 measures +6.0): compare the trace's exact extremes with the closed form, and the captures within a pixel. The navigation cube and the corner coordinate system carry red and blue marks, the same in both eyes, that join colour centroids: switch them off in rows that measure colours, and check them in a row of their own. A row longer than five minutes without input ends under KWin's screen locker, which cannot start its greeter in the rig, and from then on no window is painted: write `kscreenlockerrc` with `Autolock=false` before KWin starts (stereo-proof-rig's lessons page). In a loop that starts the program again and again inside one session, wait for that start's own pipeline by its PID: a bare `wait` also waits for KWin and never returns. Under memcheck start the real binary (`/usr/lib/freecad/bin/freecad-python3` in Debian's layout), not the `/usr/bin/freecad` link: under Valgrind `/proc/self/exe` names the link, FreeCAD takes `/usr/` for its home, and a macro fails with `No module named 'PySide'` because the `Ext` folder is not on its path.
