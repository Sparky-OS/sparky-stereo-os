# A player on the engine's video layer

Learned from ORDER 453, 453b and 453c (November 2026): Dragon Player and Plank Player, both built on Qt Multimedia's `VideoOutput` stereo layer since 7 October 2026, made to say when the desktop refuses to show a video in 3D.

## What the player keeps, what the engine does

- **The engine (Qt Multimedia's `VideoOutput` / `Video`)** takes every packing apart, declares the video's own surface to Stereo KWin as full side by side, attaches nothing until the declaration is taken, and says a refusal in `stereoLayerFailure` (a string, empty while all is well; `stereoLayerActive` is false while it is set).
  A player has no stereo layer, no declaration and no drawing of its own: a program that still carries one is a superseded recipe (see the first trap).
- **The player** keeps its menu bound to the output's properties (`stereoView`, `stereoInput`, `stereoSwapEyes`, `stereoShapeFallback`, the depth properties for Dragon), its per-file store next to the resume position (Dragon: `dragonplayerrc`, group `[StereoVideo]`, key = SHA-256 of the file's URL, `.input` for the packing, `.depth` for the depth options), the offer to save the correction into the file (Dragon), and the message.

## The refused declaration shown (the one piece a player adds)

The sentence is one for both players, in the nine languages, with the engine's own reason (English) in the brackets: "This video is shown flat: the desktop refused to show it in 3D (%1)."

- Dragon: a `Kirigami.InlineMessage` of the warning type over the video, `visible: video.stereoLayerFailure.length > 0`.
- Plank Player: a banner over the video in the theme's own warning colours (`Kirigami.Theme.neutralBackgroundColor`, `neutralTextColor` for its border, `textColor` for the text), never a fixed colour: a fixed orange ignored the person's colour scheme.
- The video itself is the ordinary picture (one view), so the person sees a picture and is told why it is flat. Nothing else changes: every input, the per-file choices and the depth options stay.
- `qmllint` of stock Qt says "Member stereoLayerFailure not found": the property exists only with Qt Multimedia +stereo3d15, so the player depends on it.

## Recipe: prove a player on the layer, red and green

1. **Two rig images.** Take lane 448's `:red` (Qt Multimedia +stereo3d14) and `:green` (+stereo3d15) rig images (Stereo KWin, a QML runtime, the NVIDIA card through CDI), and install the player's debs in a throwaway container as root with apt: the published tree as a file repository (`deb [trusted=yes] file:/repo mashtabba main`, the image's own `sparky-stereo.sources` removed), the round's debs as a flat repository (`dpkg-scanpackages -m . /dev/null > Packages` and a `Release` with `Origin: Sparky Stereo OS`, so apt keeps the +15 build), Debian over https (`https-only-apt.sh`), then `docker commit -c 'CMD ["sh"]'`.
   "Today" is the published players on +14; "yours" is the new players on +15, installed from the debs, so the Depends are proved too.
   Plank Player needs `qml6-module-org-kde-desktop`: `app/main.cpp` selects that Qt Quick Controls style before loading `main.qml`.
   The earlier rig added it explicitly because the package only recommended it; that workaround does not prove the package's dependencies.
   See [required QML modules](../../edition-packaging/references/required-qml-modules.md) for the installation test.
2. **One fresh Stereo KWin per case,** a fresh `HOME` and `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` (the per-file store of an earlier case must not decide a later one), the player started with a file argument, both eyes captured by ScreenShot2 after the video plays and again a few seconds later.
3. **The refuse case:** `LD_PRELOAD=refuse-shim.so STEREO_REFUSE=1` (the shim of the proof rig). Today: the packed frame, red left of blue, in both eyes, 0 `kde_stereo_content` requests and a buffer attached to the video's subsurface at every frame (249 and 327 in the two players' runs). Yours: the left view in both eyes, the message in the window, 0 attaches.
   Count the attaches, not the subsurfaces: the layer makes its subsurface before the declaration, so `get_subsurface` is 1 in both (`tools/subsurface_attaches.py` reads KWin's `WAYLAND_DEBUG=server` log: the surfaces made a subsurface and the buffers attached and committed to each).
4. **The controls** (same session without the shim): a clip marked left red, right blue is STEREO with 1 `create` and 1 `set_content`; the box of each view has the predicted aspect (the eye size, or the frame's for half views) and both eyes' boxes coincide within a pixel.
5. **The input choices** need input the way a person gives it. KWin's fake input protocol (`org_kde_kwin_fake_input`, with `KWIN_WAYLAND_NO_PERMISSION_CHECKS=1`) drives a pointer and a keyboard: the proof rig's `scripts/fakeinput.c`, built with `scripts/build-fakeinput.sh` and used as [input-delivery.md](../../stereo-proof-rig/references/input-delivery.md) says (steps `m:X,Y`, `b:272`, `k:KEY+KEY`, `s:MS`). Search the skills' scripts before writing a driver: this lane wrote its own 100 lines before finding that one.
   Dragon's controls bar shows only after the pointer moved a few times (six short moves 100 ms apart), its menu button is the rightmost button, and every position is read from a capture, never guessed; Alt+Shift+W is its swap shortcut.
   Plank Player's on-screen display opens on the Down key, its 3D button is the third of the four buttons at the right, and its menu is a list that keys and Space drive (the Space key activates an entry; Return does not).
6. **Per-file store and the file:** after a choice, read the player's rc file from the session's config directory; the media file's SHA-256 before and after "Save the 3D Correction into the File..." with Cancel pressed is the same (the offer writes nothing).
7. **A 2D clip** (a flat green one): 0 declarations, the capture of today's and yours differ in 0 pixels, and every entry that corrects a 3D video is greyed: open the player's 3D menu on the 2D clip and look at each entry, the last ones included (Plank Player's list scrolls, and its Swap Eyes is the last entry).

## Traps

- **Symptom:** a player on the video layer exits with status 134 and the line `Cannot make QOpenGLContext current in a different thread` the moment the next file is opened (ORDER 496: Plank Player and Dragon Player with a half side by side, or a top and bottom, file; no abort with a full side by side file or with `QSG_RENDER_LOOP=basic`), on an NVIDIA machine, with Qt Multimedia +stereo3d14 and +stereo3d15.
  Cause: not the stereo patches and not the packing. Qt's own CUDA texture converter (Tim Blechmann, Qt dev c57f4af5ab, carried as patch 0033) keeps GL textures registered with CUDA, and its destructor made the QRhi's context current to delete them. The converter lives in the decoder's frames context, so it dies on the thread that lets go the last reference to that context. `QMediaPlayer::setSource` closes the decoder on the GUI thread (`PlaybackEngine::~PlaybackEngine`, `avcodec_free_context`, `av_buffer_unref`), and if the scene graph's render thread has already dropped its last frame, the GUI thread is the last one: `QOpenGLContext::makeCurrent` off the context's thread is `qFatal`.
  Which thread is last depends on how long the render thread keeps frames, so treat it as a race: a plain QML `Video` survives a half side by side switch (the render thread is last), Plank and Dragon with a half or a top and bottom packing abort every time, with full side by side they do not (gdb: the converter dies on the render thread there, on thread 1 in the others).
  Fix (patch 0047, in the engine, not in a player and never `QSG_RENDER_LOOP=basic`): make the context current only on the context's own thread; on another thread give the planes (texture and CUDA registration) to the context's share group with a `QOpenGLSharedResource` subclass, which Qt frees at the next `makeCurrent` or `doneCurrent` of a context in the group (`deletePendingResources`, once per frame in the scene graph), holding a reference to the decoder's `AVHWDeviceContext` so the CUDA context is still there then.
  How found: `gdb -batch -ex run -ex 'thread apply all bt'` over the player in the rig (the probe's `LD_PRELOAD` goes through `set exec-wrapper env LD_PRELOAD=...`), the dbgsym debs of the package installed in a copy of the rig image: thread 1 read `~CUDAGLTextureConverter` under `HwFrameContextData` under `avcodec_free_context` under `QMediaPlayer::setSource`. Breakpoints on the destructor and on `createTextureHandles`, printing `$_thread`, showed the converter made on the render thread in every packing and destroyed on the render thread (full side by side) or on thread 1 (half).
  Trap inside the fix: the first fix unregistered the CUDA resource at once on the GUI thread and deferred only the texture. It passed and did not abort, and `cuGraphicsUnregisterResource` returned 219 (`CUDA_ERROR_INVALID_GRAPHICS_CONTEXT`) every time, a result the converter never read: the registration leaked. CUDA wants the OpenGL context of the texture current for that call (CUDA C++ Programming Guide, OpenGL interoperability: "The OpenGL context whose resources are being shared has to be current to the host thread making any OpenGL interoperability API calls"). Read the result with a Python `gdb.FinishBreakpoint` on `cuGraphicsUnregisterResource` (`$rax` at the return; `finish` inside a breakpoint's command list stops the list), and the converter now says a failed unregistration.
  Test: `tests/auto/unit/plugins/multimedia/ffmpeg/cudatextureconverter` makes a converter on a render thread with its own QRhi, takes a CUDA frame from a frames context (`HWAccel::create(AV_HWDEVICE_TYPE_CUDA)`, `av_hwframe_get_buffer`, the converter placed in `HwFrameContextData` as the decoder does), and lets the frames context go on the render thread and on another thread. Red is exit 134 with the same line; green checks with `glIsTexture` after the next `makeCurrent` that the textures are gone and with `QTest::failOnWarning` that no unregistration failed. Release the device after the converter in a test, never before: a converter that outlives its device pushes a CUDA context that is gone (a segmentation fault in `cuGraphicsUnregisterResource`). It runs in a KWin session with the NVIDIA card (`QT_QPA_PLATFORM=wayland`) and skips without CUDA.
  Proof shape: A (the packing picked in the 3D menu), B (flat), A, B, A under the default loop for each of the four packings (side by side and top and bottom, half and full), the process alive after every switch, both eyes read after every A (left red, right blue), then Dragon the same way.
  VA-API (ORDER 496b, AMD radeonsi, Mesa 26.1.6): the same destructor pattern in `VAAPITextureHandles::~VAAPITextureHandles` does not abort at the next file, and nothing was changed. The handles are not held by the decoder's frames context, as the CUDA converter is: `QFFmpegVideoBuffer::mapTextures` hands them to its caller, and the callers are `QVideoFrameTexturePool` (owned by `QSGVideoNode` on the render thread, by `QVideoWindow` on the GUI thread that has its own context) and `createTextures` replacing the old handles, so they die on the thread that made them, the one that runs the QRhi. gdb with a breakpoint on `createTextureHandles` and on the destructor, `$_thread` printed: Plank Player, all four packings, 74 to 196 handles made and every destructor call on the render thread, none on thread 1; a bare QML `Video`, 51 and the same; 40 switches at 500 ms per packing, exit 0; Plank A, B, A, B, A with both eyes right in every packing. Dragon Player on the same stack decodes with VA-API but its output takes the frames from memory (`Handle type: NoHandle`, `createTexturesFromMemory`), so no handle is ever made there. Not run: Intel (iHD), and a destructor on the GUI thread (it would abort the same way: `QOpenGLContext::makeCurrent` checks the thread before it looks at the surface). If a new consumer ever keeps a frame's textures on another thread, give `VAAPITextureHandles` the share-group release of patch 0047.
- **Recipe for the VA-API rig (ORDER 496b):** the rig image plus `mesa-va-drivers` and `vainfo` (apt over https, the edition's Mesa wins), the AMD render node only (`--device /dev/dri/renderD128`, the render group by number) and no NVIDIA, KWin on the same card (`GBM_BACKEND` not set), `vainfo --display drm --device /dev/dri/renderD128` first (radeonsi, `VAProfileH264High : VAEntrypointVLD`), then `QT_LOGGING_RULES='qt.multimedia.ffmpeg.hwaccel*=true;qt.multimedia.video*=true'` in the player: `Checking HW context: vaapi`, `Creating VAAPI HW accelerator` and `Handle type: RhiTextureHandle` say the zero-copy route is on; `createTexturesFromMemory` says the frames travel through memory and the converter is not in play.
- **Symptom:** a probe driver stops with "Probe did not answer" at a source change, the player is alive and its GUI thread idles in the event loop (gdb `thread apply all bt`), now and then and never twice in the same place.
  Cause: the probe preloaded into the player writes its answer to stderr, and so does FFmpeg's log; when the two writes meet the answer is glued to the end of a half-written log line ("DAR 16:9], PLAYERS_PROBE {...}"), and a reader that looks for lines starting with `PLAYERS_PROBE ` never sees it.
  Fix: find `PLAYERS_PROBE ` anywhere in the line (`tools/proof.py` of the lane). How found: the answer was in `client.log`, one line after the stack of the "hung" player, with a prefix.
- **Symptom:** a gdb breakpoint `file.cpp:LINE` on a function of the packaged plugin stays pending and never hits, and `this` prints as `<optimized out>`.
  Cause: the packages are Lean Code builds, so the comments are stripped and the line numbers are not the source's (`info line` says "out of range"); the optimized destructor has no `this`.
  Fix: break on the function name (`break QFFmpeg::(anonymous namespace)::VAAPITextureHandles::~VAAPITextureHandles`; `info functions NAME` on the library shows the name), and get a context with a call through the mangled name (`((long(*)())_ZN14QOpenGLContext14currentContextEv)()`).
- **Symptom:** a brief says "move the players onto the shared video layer, removing their copies", and the lane finds both already on it.
  Cause: the build tree keeps the superseded +stereo3d1 and +stereo3d2 recipes (`upstream/dragonplayer`, `upstream/plank-player`) beside the shipped ones (`upstream/qtmultimedia/...` and `packages/depth-source`); a folder in the tree is not proof of what ships.
  Fix: before a brief or a change, list the published source's `debian/patches/series` and changelog (`tar tf` of the `.debian.tar.xz` in the pool) and name the shipped recipe's folder; mark a superseded folder with a README that says where the shipped recipe is.
  How found: the pool's Dragon series (0001 to 0008 and the po patch) had no layer of its own.
- **Symptom:** the player's window is black in a control run, 12 to 20 seconds after the start, and `get_subsurface` is 1 with a declaration.
  Cause: the clip was 10 seconds long and the player had reached its end; the layer is empty after it.
  Fix: clips of two minutes, captures within 15 seconds. How found: the 2D clip's last frame stayed green while the 3D clips went black, then `ffprobe`'s duration.
- **Symptom:** `build.sh dragon` ends with "conflicting supplied package: libqt6multimedia6; provide one authoritative edition snapshot" after a good compile.
  Cause: the link gate supplies the published +stereo3d14 from the siblings list and the +stereo3d15 of the round from the flat repository.
  Fix: `ROUND_SOURCES=qt6-multimedia` (the sources built earlier in the round are taken out of the published list). How found: the gate's one line, after `dh_builddeb`.
- **Symptom:** `plank-player` prints `module "org.kde.desktop" is not installed` and no window opens in the rig image.
  Cause: its `Depends` name the Bigscreen module but not the Qt Quick Controls desktop style (`qml6-module-org-kde-desktop`), which a Plasma desktop always has.
  Fix in Plank `+stereo3d4`: add it to `Depends` in the shipped successor generator and the standalone template. Prove installation with recommendations off and no preinstalled style; see the packaging skill. `apt-cache policy` identifies the module name; it is not `qqc2-desktop-style`.
- **Symptom:** after adding `breeze-icon-theme` and `plasma-integration` to the rig image to see the icon-only buttons, Dragon's controls bar never appeared again under the same fake pointer moves.
  Cause: the KDE platform theme changed the application's behaviour (and the window decoration), so the rig no longer measured the package. Fix: no icon theme; the buttons are found by position and read from a capture of the menu they open.
- **Symptom:** Plank Player's 3D menu shows Swap Eyes enabled on a flat 2D clip, while Dragon greys it.
  Cause: the entry was listed with `needsStereo: false` (the Input Format entries are meant to stay open to a video that does not say it is 3D, and the swap was written beside them).
  Fix: `needsStereo: true` for the swap: a video that says nothing becomes 3D when the person picks its Input Format, and the entry is enabled from then on (Plank patch 0004).
  How found: the menu captured on the 2D clip and scrolled to its last entry; the same capture on the fixed build shows it greyed.
- **Symptom:** Dragon's "Save the 3D Correction into the File..." entry is enabled on a 2D clip. It is not a correction for that clip.
  Investigation of the `+stereo3d10` source found that `ControlsBar.qml::canOfferSave` already checks stereo content, a changed correction, the view and a local file.
  A minimal test on its Qt Quick Controls runtime reproduced a `Kirigami.Action` with `visible: false` whose generated menu item remained visible and enabled after opening the menu.
  Check `menu.itemAt(index).visible` with the menu open; a closed menu makes all its children's effective visibility false and gives a misleading pass.
  Dragon `+stereo3d11` uses an explicit `QQC2.MenuItem` with visibility, enabled state and hidden height bound to the existing `canOfferSave` predicate; both the question function and the save function guard that same predicate. Keep Input Format usable on 2D content so unmarked stereo can be identified. Check the open 2D menu, then the corrected 3D offer and its Cancel path; the offer itself never writes the media.
- **Symptom:** `STEPS= run case ...` in a POSIX shell function did not reach the case script. Cause: a prefix assignment before a function call is not exported. Fix: `export` the names inside the function.
- **Symptom:** `docker commit -c 'ENTRYPOINT []'` left `["sh"]` as the entry point and later `docker run IMAGE sh -c ...` failed with "sh: 0: cannot open sh". Cause: the image was built from a container started with `--entrypoint sh`. Fix: start the build container with the image's default entry point and `sh -c` as its command, and commit with `-c 'CMD ["sh"]'`.

## Per-file corrections without a resume store

Plank Player `+stereo3d3` reset both corrections for every source and had no resume-position store. `+stereo3d4` uses QtCore's QSettings with the organisation and application names already set by `app/main.cpp`: `PlankPlayer` and `PlankPlayer`. No framework was added for this key-value store.

The file is `$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/PlankPlayer/PlankPlayer.conf`, normally `~/.config/PlankPlayer/PlankPlayer.conf`. The isolated run observed `/run/user/1000/config/PlankPlayer/PlankPlayer.conf`. Its `[StereoVideo]` group uses the same key scheme as Dragon: SHA-256 of `QUrl::toEncoded()` for the eye swap and that hash plus `.input` for the packing. The two applications use their own configuration files. There is no Plank resume position to put beside these keys, and no xattr, sidecar or file beside the media.

On every source change, load that URL's choices. Save a change only for its URL. Returning packing to Automatic removes `.input`; switching swap off removes the unsuffixed key. A failed QSettings read or sync produces an on-screen message naming the settings file, translated into the nine required languages. Making the configuration directory unwritable in the isolated run produced that visible message with the actual path.

The package proof used actual menu activation, file A / file B / back to A, then a new application process. On the default render loop, an unmarked full-side-by-side A with explicit full-side-by-side packing and eye swap lost both choices in `+stereo3d3` and restored both in `+stereo3d4`. B stayed automatic and unswapped. Clearing both choices left the config file empty. Media SHA-256, size, modification/change times and xattrs were unchanged. The half-side-by-side variant also passed with an explicitly selected basic render loop; its default threaded-loop source transition exposed an additional OpenGL context-affinity failure in both package versions, so that variant is not evidence of a successful threaded transition.

## QML diagnostics: cause and proof

- `PlayerPage.qml: ReferenceError: audioOutput is not defined`: the controls-bar `WheelHandler` refers to `audioOutput` outside the player's object scope. Upstream [merge request 86](https://invent.kde.org/multimedia/dragon/-/merge_requests/86), by Erich Seifert, contains commit `ae56fa67c9d5ff4742136a25b0071d18a4f7f047`. Dragon `+stereo3d11` carries the complete patch, with its author and upstream origin in the DEP-3 header. It binds to `player.audioOutput.volume` and restores the binding when scrolling ends, matching the video area's existing handler. `Applied-Upstream: no` records that the MR was open when checked on 10 November 2026. Test a scroll from a nonzero volume and another after an external volume change; absence of the diagnostic alone is insufficient.
- `OverlayPopup.qml: ReferenceError: backgroundSource is not defined`: the effect's visibility reads an undeclared name. Bind it to `shaderSource.sourceItem !== null`, the property of its actual ShaderEffectSource. Verify the source and visible background with the popup open.
- `IconToolButton.qml: Unable to assign [undefined] to QString`: `(action as Kirigami.Action)?.tooltip` yields `undefined` for a button without that action type, while `ToolTip.text` requires a string. Keep the typed action when present; use its tooltip, or the button's own text for a directly labelled button. Exercise action-backed and direct buttons and inspect their tooltip text, not just the log.

The latter two failures had no matching upstream fix in the audited history, merge requests or issue search. They remain unfiled; the edition carries its own patches and sends nothing to KDE.

The installed `+stereo3d11` rig run had zero occurrences of those three diagnostics. Native compositor wheel input over the controls bar changed volume from 0.80 to 0.95 and, after setting 0.40 externally, to 0.55; `+stereo3d10` jumped to 0.15 and then 0.30. Play/pause changed playback state, the direct forward button sought five seconds, the action-backed and directly labelled tooltips were strings, and the opened popup had a non-null source with its effect visible. A corrected 3D clip still opened the Save question; cancelling left the media hash unchanged. On 2D, the actual Save item was hidden, disabled and zero height, both entry functions returned without a dialog or write, Swap stayed disabled, and Input Format remained enabled.

For the proof driver, take the window position from KWin and add Qt's client-side frame margins when translating an item's coordinates on Wayland. A raw `mapToGlobal()` alone does not establish the compositor's global position. Scroll over a tool button on the controls bar when testing its WheelHandler: the seek slider consumes the event. Inspect a ShaderEffectSource through its source and effect properties, not a presumed visual parent; internal effect objects may have no QML context and must not be passed to a QML expression evaluator.
